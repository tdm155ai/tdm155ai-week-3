# This week's practice (no AI)

No AI this week. Pick one:

## Option A: photos with attention to light

A set of photographs, six to ten, where you chose the light on purpose. For each one, be able to say: which source is the key, whether it's hard or soft, where the catchlight is, and whether there's a rim. A window, a desk lamp, a phone screen, and a borrowed panel all count as sources. Shoot the same face or object under two different setups if you want a contrasting pair.

## Option B: level up the selfie video

A short selfie video that uses what you did at the phone station: the tripod at eye level, exposure and white balance locked in a manual app, the mic close (the wireless mic, or a wired earbud mic held near the mouth). Do it once the old way and once the new way if you want a contrasting pair.

## The standing turn-in

The weekly turn-in is still a video: two short tutorials, or two contrasting shots or scenes. Obvious pairs from today: the same subject through the acrylic vs. straight from the phone; the small panel far away vs. the big panel close; the gimbal walk vs. the handheld walk.

## Borrow gear

Light panels, the pocket RGB panel, tripods, the gimbal, and the wireless mics can be borrowed from the Learning Lab for the week. Ask at the end of class or in the course Slack channel. The [desk video kit](05-desk-video-kit.md) page is the take-home version of stations 1 and 4.
